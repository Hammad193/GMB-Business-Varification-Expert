Google's Strict Verification
Policies Explained

Google has made GBP verification policies stricter than ever to prevent fake listings, spam, and misleading business information. This means businesses must now prove they are legitimate and trustworthy before Google allows their profile to go live on Maps and local search results.

Why Google Tightened Verification

🚫

Spam Control

Fake businesses were using loopholes to flood Google Maps with false listings.

🛡️

User Safety

Google wants customers to trust that every business shown is real and reliable.

⭐

Quality Standards

Only businesses with accurate information and compliance get visibility.

What Google Looks For in Verification

📍

A real physical location (for storefronts) or a properly defined service area (for SABs).

📞

Consistent business details (name, address, phone number) across the web.

📸

Evidence of legitimate operations, such as signage, office setup, or tools shown in video verification.

📋

Businesses following Google's compliance rules for their category and services.

⚠️ Impact on Businesses

These strict policies mean even genuine businesses can face:

  • ❌ Rejected video calls due to minor mistakes.
  • ❌ Postcards never arriving or failing to activate.
  • ❌ Account flagged or suspended if Google finds mismatched or incomplete details.
